## Goal
Turn LGBTQ+ centre partnerships into a revenue channel that also stocks their closets: each centre gets a Virtual Closet page; its community buys with an automatic discount on one order, applies for free pairs, or sponsors the closet; everything lands in one box that RUBIES matches dollar for dollar. Grew out of the retailer affiliate idea, redirected at orgs after the Uniting Pride call (2026-09-11). Full record and every decision: `.claude/plans/org-closet-programme.md` (read its "Where things stand" summary first).

## Current Status
**Where it stands (2026-09-22).** Live in link mode at closet.rubyshines.com (its own Railway service, `virtual-closet/`; the domain answers on https). Each centre gets a page with one shop button (20% off once per customer, a hidden code per tap), four sponsor tiles, the five styles and a fundraiser bar of lifetime raised against a goal (1,000 by default). Its balance is a quarter of what buyers paid plus sponsor money at face value, spent by email to Jamie on partner orders at the country rate (US and Australia 50%, everywhere else 30%, $300 USD minimum) and debited by tool. Each centre's money is in its own currency, set by its country; an order paid in another currency settles once at the store's fx-reference rate. Emails: welcome from Jamie (QR and table-tent sign attached), a digest from care@ on days with activity, a sponsor thank-you. Store side: the shop route carries the closet to rubyshines.com; an order without a code credits the centre within 30 days of the tap, first order per email; the closet bar and cart line are on preview theme `rubies-ecom-v4/wt/virtual-closet-store`, not on the live theme. Centres: The Attic Youth Center (Philadelphia, #9, USD) active, one real order credited and its first digest sent 2026-09-22; Lumenus (Toronto, #11, CAD) enrolled 2026-09-22 as the currency test centre; the RUBIES Test Closet (#10) retired. Test orders pay with the gift card noted in `.claude/plans/virtual-closet-theme.md`.

**Next, in order:** (1) Jamie's phone test on the preview theme, then merge the theme; (2) a real sponsorship through a live page and the digest that follows (never done; The Attic's only real row is a code order); (3) decide the matching claim for 30% centres (parked); (4) Uniting Pride for October Pride Fest; (5) the order confirmation email line (parked). ## Decisions Made
- 2026-09-22 — Each centre keeps its money in its own currency, set from its country at enrolment (US USD, CA CAD, GB GBP, Eurozone EUR, AU AUD; no override, no centre-facing setting). An order paid in that currency records as paid; anything else settles once from Shopify's USD shop money at the fx-reference rate for the centre's country, and the row keeps what was paid and the rate. Sponsor tiles are fixed round prices per Shopify market, so a sponsor pays £25, never a converted £21. A non-USD centre's page shows no style prices; the store shows its market's own.
- 2026-09-22 — The welcome quotes partner pricing from the wholesale rule (country rate, $300 USD order minimum), never a fixed line. Open: "RUBIES matches it" on the page and the thank-you assumes the 50% rate and is untrue for a 30% centre (parked).
- 2026-09-22 — The 20% is a product discount on the "virtual-closet-eligible" collection (every product except the sponsorship product and gift cards), with the same combination flags as every managed discount. It therefore follows the store's rule for product discounts: a shopper gets the better of the closet 20% and any sale or volume tier, never both, and it combines with order discounts (Smile rewards) and shipping discounts. Aligned live with `setupShopify.js --discount --create`.
- 2026-09-22 — A closet code is removed from Shopify the moment its order is credited. Shopify counts uses per discount, not per code, so a per-tap code otherwise stays usable by anyone who saw it in checkout; our row keeps it for attribution.
- 2026-09-22 — Credit is the first order only, within 30 days of the tap; the "customer year" from the 09-19 draft is dropped. The store shows closet surfaces only to a shopper who came from a closet link. The used-20% message is shown on the cart for the same device and for a signed-in customer; a guest on a new device gets Shopify's own message at checkout (a checkout-webhook path was considered and not built). One shared discount, never one per centre, because once-per-customer is enforced per discount.
- 2026-09-22 — Sponsor tiles are variants of the one hidden product, not quantities of a $1 unit; the sponsor line names the centre through a visible line property.
- 2026-09-21 — Every centre has a goal the running total is drawn against, $1,000 unless the centre tells Jamie otherwise; lifetime raised, never reset. Jamie sets it by tool; no centre-facing setting.
- 2026-09-21 — Cut the pilot back to an affiliate-shaped programme because the risk is execution (for the centre and for RUBIES), not build cost: a link, a balance and an email. Terms: 20% off once per customer, applied automatically; the centre earns 25% of what buyers pay plus sponsor dollars at face value; credit is spent at partner pricing (the rate by country, $300 USD minimum after discount; at 50% that is $600 retail), which is the match; ordering stays manual by email to Jamie. Wording: one mechanism word, "match", on the page's total line; "a quarter" for buyers; the digit 25% only in the centre's welcome. The welcome comes from Jamie, the digest and sponsor thank-you from care@. Record: `.claude/plans/org-closet-programme.md`, "The minimal cut".
- 2026-09-18 — Stage 1 review applied to the app (record: `.claude/plans/virtual-closet-handover/virtual-closet-stage1-review.md`). Two rule changes: an approved request always joins the open box and the goal grows to cover it (the wait list only ever held requests made against a funded box the centre had not sent, which stranded them; sending opens the next box so later requests land there); and the request form offers each style its own size run (AJ, Charlie, Brooke and Ruby run in kids numbers then L to 3X, with XS, S and M meaning the 12, 14 and 16 on the shelf; Sassy runs XS to 4X). The 30-day attribution line came off Home and Offer details until the theme script exists (parked).
- 2026-09-18 — Sign-up is self-service with the operator approving after; the donation programme is called Pass It On; sizes are the store's (no XL); the 20% is once per customer, new or returning; add-to-the-box is paid on the spot; ordering outside the box stays by email; the operator side lives in the CS dashboard; accounts are the service's own tables.
- 2026-09-16 — No visible code: the discount is applied at checkout from the centre's link; a 30-day browser window from the link credits later orders to the closet (credit only). First order only for both discount and credit. Superseded 2026-09-18: one order per customer, existing customers included.
- 2026-09-16 — One box open per centre, sequential; goal is the larger of the centre's target and the cost of approved requests; wait list for the shipment after; centre chooses send-now past the goal. No time-based auto-ship.
- 2026-09-16 — Requests carry the name they go by, items, pickup or ship (address collected for shipping, held by RUBIES only), a sentence on what a pair would mean, and an opt-in to share it anonymously.
- 2026-09-16 — Box menu limited to the five highest-margin, deepest-stock styles; gaff, Stella and basic chest pads excluded (negative or thin at the matched price).
- 2026-09-14 — Virtual first; the physical lobby stand and serialised kits are parked behind the virtual programme.
- 2026-09-14 — Orgs will not maintain a tool: the page runs on RUBIES data; the centre's jobs are share the link, set limits, fill the box.
- 2026-09-14 — Sponsorship is a product sale, not a donation, and there is no wallet; money in a box is matched, never held for a named person.
