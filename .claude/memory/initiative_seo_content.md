## Goal
Drive organic traffic and revenue through SEO optimization and content.

## Phases
1. SEO tracking pipelines — complete (daily GSC, GA4, Shopify, weekly digest)
2. Content execution — runs as the weekly `/marketing-weekly` loop: evidence from search data, customer language and seasonality → 2-3 topic candidates → an approval package for the creative director. Nothing publishes without approval.
3. On-page SEO — `seo_meta_draft` / `seo_meta_update` tools for collection and product meta in house style (see domain_inventory_catalog.md)

## Current Status
Tracking infrastructure built and running. Content runs on the weekly loop when invoked; blog posts are registered in Supabase so topics are not repeated.

## Decisions Made
- Blog writing is community-first plus SEO traffic: brand voice for tone, real reviews for social proof (domain_marketing.md).
