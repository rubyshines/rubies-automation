## What's Built

**Prospect Discovery Pipeline:** Google Maps searches across tier-1/tier-2 cities with targeted search terms (LGBTQ friendly boutique, swimwear shop, etc.). Deduplicates by Google Place ID and website domain.

**Scraping Pipeline:** Puppeteer scrapes homepage + key pages (about, shop, contact) for each prospect with a website, with browser recycling and rate limiting.

**Contact Finder:** Regex + DOM parsing extracts email, phone, contact form URL. Classifies email type (business/personal) for scoring.

**AI Analysis:** Claude analyzes scraped HTML into structured JSON: subcategory, trans/gender-affirming mentions, product type, ownership (independent vs chain), presence, brand list, outreach angle.

**Lead Scoring:** Points-based 1-10 scale from the analysis signals (trans mention, LGBTQ/inclusivity, gender products, underwear/swimwear, independent, physical store; chain and no-website penalised). A threshold score marks a prospect qualified.

**Google Sheets Sync:** Exports qualified prospects for sales outreach.

**Wholesale Orders (MCP tool):** Two-phase confirmation like exchanges. Pricing defaults by country/zone (US/AU 50% off, others 30% off, free shipping, AU auto-splits at the de minimis threshold). Negotiated per-partner terms (discount %, incoterms DDP/DDU, forced currency) live on `b2b_companies.wholesale_*`, matched by customer email (`customer-service/lib/wholesaleTerms.js`); precedence is explicit param > stored terms > country/zone default.

**Store Locator (MCP tools):** `store_locator_*` tools (list/create/update/delete/publish) manage the rubyshines.com/pages/store-locator map. Data lives in `b2b_companies` (`locator_*` columns + `on_store_locator` flag). Publish writes `rubies-ecom-v4/assets/store-locators.json` via worktree + auto-merge, same pattern as donation partners. Haiku auto-extracts store descriptions from the website.

**Outreach Engine:** the unified B2B outreach system (code is truth for what's built; the original design plan was retired). One spine, three channels (retailers / LGBTQ+ orgs / affiliates): 6-tier signal-based queue, cadence engine (per-message-type due conditions), two Opus advisors (`b2b_sales_advisor`, `b2b_community_advisor`) drafting into `b2b_drafts` with an enforced output schema (facts_to_verify + open_commitments fields instead of "don't hallucinate" rules), Gmail reply correlation (inbound → company thread, bounce/departure detection pauses cadence), and a two-phase send tool **hard-gated by the `b2b_send_enabled` system flag (default OFF; go-live is a Jamie-only act)**. Surfaces: operator-console tools (`b2b_queue`, `b2b_draft`, `send_b2b_email`) + dashboard Outreach panel. Tables: `b2b_threads` / `b2b_messages` / `b2b_drafts` / `b2b_contacts` / `b2b_meetings` + outreach columns on `b2b_companies`. Engine outbound `b2b_messages` rows are written by the send tool; manual Gmail sends are reconciled in as `source='manual_send'`.

**Panel truth layer:** queue and history are kept honest against Gmail. `manualSendReconcile.js` backfills messages the engine didn't send (manual Gmail replies as `manual_send`, pre-correlation inbound as `gmail_backfill`), idempotent on `gmail_message_id`; runs on queue/history fetch. Reply-rate/A-B metrics count only `source='send_tool'` rows. Closed threads never produce Tier-1 "waiting on us". `b2b-outreach/sync/syncB2bCompanyState.js` (daily-sync-all sub-pipeline, after Orders) keeps `b2b_companies` order fields, `relationship_state` promotions, and org `program_flags` true from the orders mirror + donation_partners registry.

**Relationship summary:** `b2b-outreach/lib/relationshipSummary.js` keeps a per-company rolling recap + suggested next step on `b2b_companies` (`relationship_*` columns), rebuilt from `b2b_messages` (incremental on append, full rebuild when messages land behind the watermark). Sweeps nightly in `daily-sync-all`; `POST /api/b2b/companies/:id/summary/refresh` backs the panel's refresh. Surfaced in the panel, the `b2b_search` console tool, and the advisor's context. The Outreach detail pane is built around it: "Where this stands" above the composer, the conversation beneath as audit trail.

**Thread discovery sweep:** `sweepEmptyCompanies` in `manualSendReconcile.js` searches Gmail nightly for companies holding zero `b2b_messages`, across every address on file, in `daily-sync-all` immediately before Relationship Summaries. Bounded per run, prioritising companies claiming a relationship they cannot evidence, stamped with `threads_discovered_at` so companies with no history back off.

**Browse surfaces:** the Outreach panel has five sidebar modes: **Queue** (what's due, the 6-tier view), **To do** and **On Me** (the commitments list, see below), **Activity** (`GET /api/b2b/activity`, every message newest-first), **Companies** (`GET /api/b2b/companies`, searchable directory filtered on relationship stage and conversation state independently). Search spans company, contact and thread-subject fields and returns which matched. Closed threads carry Reopen and Close. The same `queueService.js` functions back the panel and the `b2b_search` / `b2b_activity` / `b2b_reopen_thread` console tools.

**Meeting scheduling:** a Schedule control in the Outreach panel reads Jamie's three calendars and either shows availability (read-only) or books the call: creates `RUBIES x <Company>` with a Meet link, invites the contact, and sends the reply in one action. Agent-agnostic tools: `calendar_availability`, `read_proposed_times`, `schedule_meeting`. Calendar auth is a SEPARATE OAuth token from Gmail's (re-minting Gmail's risks losing `gmail.modify` and breaking CS intake). Meetings live in `b2b_meetings`; a booked call suppresses the cadence. Rehearsal mirrors `test_send`: real event, real invite, to Jamie only, writing nothing. Moving and cancelling (2026-09-10): a company with an upcoming booked call plus a picked slot is a MOVE of that call by definition, so no detection is needed — the existing Google event is patched (Google sends the update, the Meet link survives), the row moves in place, and the reply says "I moved our call"; `new_call` is the escape hatch for a genuine second call. Cancel deletes the event (Google tells them) and sends nothing of ours. The message read also reports `wants_to_reschedule`, shown on the panel's booked line. Grouping: Jamie stacks calls, so the slot engine (`availability.js`, deterministic) scores free slots by how tightly they sit against something already booked that day and returns `bestFits`; the panel leads with those over a week grid, and `calendar_availability` leads its summary with them so every advisor offers the same times.

**Commitments (2026-09-10):** `b2b_commitments` is the structured list of what Jamie owes and is waiting on: one row per promise (owner `me`|`them`, company optional, text, due date, open/done), fed by a call's Next Steps, by the relationship summariser reading mail (its output carries `commitments` + `settled`), and by the operator. `b2b-outreach/lib/commitments.js` is the one write path (dedupe on owner + normalised text against open rows) and the only writer of the derived `b2b_companies.on_me_*` columns. On Me is a query over it. Surfaces: Outreach sidebar modes **To do** (rows are promises) and **On Me** (grouped by company), a You owe / They owe block under "Where this stands" with "Done, write to them" (the send completes the item and links the message), console tool `b2b_commitments`. Backfill: `b2b-outreach/sync/backfillCommitments.js`.

**Meeting notes (2026-09-10):** the server holds its own Wispr Flow OAuth grant (`b2b-outreach/lib/wisprClient.js`, MCP over HTTP; token in the `oauth_tokens` row, minted once by `scripts/authWispr.js`). `b2b-outreach/lib/meetingNotes.js` finds a call's recording by every linked calendar event id, else by start time + title, stores summary/transcript/link on the `b2b_meetings` row, marks it held, lifts the Next Steps onto the commitments list and rebuilds the recap. Runs nightly (`Meeting Notes` step, after Calendar Meetings) and on Held; `b2b_meeting_notes` console tool fetches one call or records notes by hand. The panel's Calls block shows the notes folded with a Wispr link.

**Company location and timezone:** every company carries `city`, `region`, `country` and a stored `timezone` + `timezone_source` (`operator` | `inferred`). `b2b-outreach/lib/companyLocation.js` is the one answer to "their timezone" for every surface: an operator-set zone wins, else the location decides deterministically, else an earlier inference. Editable in the sidebar and the Schedule panel; `b2b_update_company` console tool; `b2b_add_prospect` takes city/region at intake; `b2b-outreach/sync/backfillCompanyTimezones.js` keeps the stored column in step. Phone numbers were rejected for inference (scraped values are mostly not real numbers).

**Calendar-driven meetings:** Google Calendar is the source of truth for every call. `b2b-outreach/lib/meetingSync.js` reads the organizer calendar nightly in `daily-sync-all` ("Calendar Meetings") and live from the Gmail push handler on any `calendar_notice`, resolving attendees to companies through `b2b-outreach/lib/companyMatch.js` (the same resolution inbound mail uses) and upserting `b2b_meetings` on `(company_id, google_event_id)`, including partner-booked calls (`booked_by='partner'`), moves, cancellations and RSVPs. Operator facts on a row are never overwritten. The operator records attendance as `outcome` (`held` | `no_show`); a no-show lands the deterministic `missed_call` reschedule template. Surfaces: the panel's Calls block, `b2b_meeting_outcome`. The Gmail intake stores the iCalendar METHOD off any `text/calendar` part (`email_messages.calendar_method`). Wispr Flow Notetaker records the calls (its `calendar_id` is the Google event id); notes ingest not yet built. Design record: `.claude/memory/project_meeting_sync.md`.

**Post-call follow-up + composer templates:** the day after a booked call ends with no outbound since, the company surfaces at Tier 1 (`post_call_followup`, deliberately NOT an initiating type: no AI draft, ever) until anything goes out to them or the operator dismisses it onto the meeting row. The composer's empty state offers operator templates (`b2b-outreach/lib/messageTemplates.js`): `setup_call`, `partner_onboarding` (orgs only: agreement spec + onboarding survey link + call-notes placeholder), `missed_call`, and `waiting_in_room` (offered only while a booked call is in progress; a fresh email with the Meet link for a contact who has not joined, also a one-click button on the panel's Calls block). Applied templates land in the pending compose row (`advisor: null`) with `template_id`/`template_body` in `structured`. Console tools: `b2b_template`, `b2b_dismiss_post_call`. Design record: `.claude/plans/b2b-post-call-followup-templates.md`.

**Wholesale line sheet:** `b2b-outreach/lib/wholesalePriceList.js` is the one source for retailer terms and pricing: a curated featured set (`FEATURED`), title-based sections, variants grouped by price into youth/adult bands, wholesale = retail less the discount, sections ranked by 12-month units sold at publish. Two surfaces read it: the `wholesale_terms` composer template (retailers only; terms inline at the company's rate, a link to the page carrying their country, no attachment) and rubyshines.com/pages/wholesale-pricing (unlisted, `noindex`), a theme section that reads prices, photos and names live from the Shopify catalog in Liquid and applies the rate on the page, so a retail change never touches the theme. `wholesale_price_list_publish` lands only what Shopify does not know (`assets/wholesale-pricing.json`: featured handles per section in popularity order, per-country views with rate + terms, the local-currency map), through the GitHub Contents API when `GITHUB_TOKEN` is set (the nightly `daily-sync-all` step) or the theme-asset worktree + PR flow locally; a no-op unless that data changed. `wholesale_price_list` prints the sheet for the console.

**Automatic follow-up ladder:** `b2b-outreach/lib/autoFollowUp.js` drafts fixed-text `followup_1`/`followup_2` rungs as replies in the thread and schedules them into the recipient's mid-morning behind pre-send guards (Gmail re-check for a reply, address health, daily cap). Due rungs and scheduled drafts are excluded from the operator queue (panel, badge, console): a rung still unscheduled a business day past due, or a scheduled draft past its slot, returns badged stuck. The ladder ends by retiring a lead or, for an active relationship, handing it to On Me with a cadence-written note.

**Bounce recovery:** `b2b-outreach/lib/bounceRecovery.js` turns a hard bounce into queue work: parses the DSN, marks the outbound `b2b_messages` row `undelivered_at`, retires the dead contact (`bounced_at`), revives the approved text as a new pending draft at Tier 1 linked by `bounced_from_draft_id`, and clears the cadence date the failed send bought. Alternate addresses are offered in the queue reason, never used automatically. `b2b-outreach/sync/replayBounces.js` re-reads stored `email_messages` nightly in `daily-sync-all` as the catch-up for the fire-and-forget push path.

**Address verification:** Kickbox SMTP-probe verification runs where an address enters the book (`addProspect`, contact updates, org enrichment), recorded per address in `b2b_email_verifications`; `scripts/verifyB2bAddresses.js` is the catch-up sweep. Only a stored `undeliverable` blocks a send (the ladder's guard and a pre-send refusal in `sendB2bEmail`); everything else fails open. An accept-all gateway reads as deliverable and bounces afterwards, so bounce recovery stays the second line.

**Departure recovery:** `departureRecovery.js` is bounce recovery's sibling for "X is no longer with the organization, contact Y" notices: a narrow Sonnet extraction reads who left and the one address named, a deterministic gate accepts it only at the org's own domain or already on file, the change goes through `updateCompanyContact`, the answered send is marked undelivered with a reason, and an unedited initiating send is re-rendered from its locked template for the new recipient and scheduled through the ladder's mechanism. The nightly Bounce Replay sweeps departure notices too.

**Discovery import + vetting (2026-09-11):** the discovery table feeds the book. `scripts/importRetailerProspects.js` (library `b2b-outreach/lib/importProspects.js`, dry-run default) turns a `retailer_prospects` row scored 5+ into an unvetted `prospect` retailer: one row per website domain within the channel and per prospect id (idempotent), scrape artefacts dropped at intake (template placeholders, image filenames, the page's own vendors), free-mail kept and labelled, a row with no email and no contact form skipped rather than shown. The researcher's profile becomes `description`; the angle and store type go to `enrich_facts` and render in the advisor context as our own research. `addProspect` refuses a domain the channel already holds. The panel's **Vet** sidebar mode is the admission gate as a list (unvetted prospects, best score first, contact route + Kickbox verdict + angle per row; Keep / Drop through the triage endpoint; keys k, d, j, o); `b2b_vetting` is its console twin. `b2b_ab_report` reads reply rate by subject variant per message type (14-day window, bounces out, one vote per company per type, interim while any window is open).

**Inbound triage:** the "New inbound" strip closes the cold-inbound gap (`correlateInbound` can only attach mail to companies that already exist). `b2b-outreach/lib/inboundTriage.js` lists org/retailer-classified inbound matching no company, one row per identifying sender domain (free-mail senders excluded); a Haiku pass extracts org name + country + any city/region the message itself states, operator-reviewed in an editable field. Admission-gated like Tier-4, never auto-created: **Add** creates the company (`source: inbound_email`, warm, vetted) + contact and imports their Gmail thread so they surface at Tier 1 with no cold intro draft, then answers — the relationship summary and the location scrape (`enrichCompany` from `b2b-discovery/enrichOrgs.js`, extracted from the batch CLI so one-at-a-time and cohort intake enrich identically) run behind the response and the panel re-reads the company as they land; **Ignore** records a lost stub row keyed on the domain. Surfaces: strip atop the panel queue, `b2b_inbound` console tool.

**Contact management:** `b2b-outreach/lib/updateContact.js` handles replace / promote / retire / restore as single operations (`b2b_update_contact` console tool); retired contacts stay visible in the panel under a "former" group.

**Gmail read state:** `b2b-outreach/lib/readState.js` mirrors Gmail unread to "waiting on us", called from the send tool, the correlator, and a nightly sweep.

**Deferrals:** pause (indefinite, `outreach_paused_at`/`_reason`) and On Me (`on_me_at`, with `on_me_source` / `outreach_paused_source` recording whether the operator, the cadence or the engine set it) on `b2b_companies`; snooze is deprecated (read path only). Thread Close/Reopen is separate from deferrals.

## Key Decisions

- **2026-09-11 — Only a deferral a person set may hide an unanswered reply.** On Me became derived from open commitments, and the summariser reads a commitment out of incoming mail, so a reply asking for a sample kit produced a claim eleven seconds later and the queue dropped that very reply as one the operator had seen and set aside. Every machine-made claim did this. The suppression test is now the claim's provenance rather than its timestamp: `on_me_source` distinguishes operator, cadence and engine, derived in one place from the rows themselves, and only an operator claim suppresses. A derived column that flattens who acted into a single default is not a labelling problem when something downstream reads it as consent.

- **2026-09-11 — A human reply reopens a closed thread, and a due ladder rung is never operator work.** Close was the one deferral without the rule that a reply arriving after it was set always surfaces: a partner's hand-off and the new coordinator's reply sat on a closed thread invisible to Tier 1, and the read-state sweep then un-bolded them in Gmail because closed reads as nobody waiting. The correlator now flips a closed thread open on the first delivery of a human reply (never on a replay, which could undo a close made after reading it; never for machine mail), and the thank-you closer may close it straight back. Separately, rungs go due at the UTC day boundary and the draft pass runs once a day, so every chase sat in the queue for half a day with a composer inviting a hand-written version of an email the engine was about to send; the operator surfaces now drop ladder-owned rungs and bring one back badged stuck a business day past due, the same shape as the scheduled-draft rule.

- **2026-09-10 — A commitment is a row, and On Me is a query over it.** The record held promises only as prose (one next-step sentence per company, rewritten every pass), so a call's action items evaporated and "what do I owe" had no answer. The structure is the object: who owes it, to whom, by when, done or not, with its source. On Me was the same thing minus the structure (both rows in it were tasks), so it became derived: a company is on Jamie while an open row says he owes it something, and one function recomputes the old columns so every reader keeps working. The rule that keeps the list trusted: the engine may close THEIRS when a later message shows delivery, never OURS, and it adds only what someone clearly said they would do — miss one rather than invent one (the first backfill over fourteen recent conversations rightly added nothing). Meeting notes arrive by themselves through the server's own Wispr grant because a fact with a system of record is read there; a recording proves a call happened, absence proves nothing, and the same call under two calendar events is one row.

- **2026-09-10 — The wholesale page is the price list, unlisted rather than gated, and the email links it.** A PDF attachment shipped for a day and was dropped: the country-aware page (`?country=xx`, else the Shopify market) serves every case it did, including the 30% international rate and a USD/local switch that is simply a Shopify market switch through the localization form, so every figure is the store's own market price with no FX arithmetic and no rate printed. Prices are never published into the theme (Jamie: "why are the prices hardcoded"); the theme reads the catalog live and the published file holds only order, terms and currencies. No login gate because the discount is in every pitch email and the sheet is retail halved; a per-company token link is the lock if one is ever wanted. Sections come from the product title, not tags (Naomi and Sassy are tagged swimwear but are underwear), and order within a section is units sold, not a hand list. A negotiated rate the page has no view for is stated in one sentence in the email.

- **2026-09-09 — The retailer channel runs on the same locked-template treatment as orgs.** The cold intro (`intro_pitch`) is an initiating type, model-filled in one why-this-store slot with a fixed A/B subject; the re-approach to a store that received a sample kit is fully fixed text with no model. A sample kit overrides the six-month fresh-intro rule, because a store holding our samples is not a stranger. Fixed subjects are one table for both channels (`fixedSubjects.js`) and assignment happens in `generateDraftForCompany` so every draft path rotates; a referred company carries no variant because the referral belongs in the subject. Lessons: render every fact a rule depends on (the referral field was recorded for months and never shown to the advisor), and every subject says what RUBIES makes, since a subject that assumes the reader remembers something reads as junk.

- **2026-09-09 — Anything we follow up on replies inside its existing thread; reopening is a status flip.** Queue entries carry the company's newest open thread (reminder rows included), so a blank composer replies into the conversation they remember and inherits its subject. Reopen no longer drafts (continuations are operator-written). Auto-reply threads are never the right target.

- **2026-09-09 — A departure notice is a bounce with a forwarding address, and a stated address is not a guess.** Setting `contact_unknown` and stopping produced the worst panel state the engine can make (an empty composer, the departed person still primary, the notice buried in the archive). An address the org printed in its own autoresponder is accepted at the org's own domain or already on file, never free-mail or a system mailbox; the residual no-usable-address case keeps the old floor with a reason naming who left.

- **2026-09-09 — The calendar is the record of a call; attendance is an operator outcome, never a status.** Reading Google Calendar makes every path (Book & Send, Calendly, a reschedule, an RSVP in any language) produce the same `b2b_meetings` row, replacing English-only parsing of invitation email subjects: when a fact has a system of record, read it there. The engine cannot detect attendance, so the operator records `held`/`no_show` beside status rather than in it, which keeps a row's booked → moved history and makes no-shows countable (the reschedule ask stops after two). Address → company resolution is one function for mail and calendar attendees alike, because two matchers over the same question will disagree.

- **2026-09-08 — Gmail unread mirrors "waiting on us", and nothing pre-filters mail before the classifier.** Read means nobody is waiting on us: the thread is closed, or only machine mail has arrived since our last outbound (manual sends count). A person's reply keeps its bold; the engine never marks UNREAD and never touches mail it has no record of. One verdict serves the send tool, the correlator, and a nightly sweep. The intake's auto-reply verdict is passed to the classifier as a hint rather than dropping mail ahead of it, so RSVPs, departure notices and out-of-office all reach the engine.

- **2026-09-08 — Pre-engine history older than six months is no history.** The re-approach gate keys on engine outbound only. For companies the engine has never written to, last contact of any kind more than six months ago means a fresh intro on the locked template; inside six months a re-approach names the thread. Scoped to the pre-tool book so it touches no running cadence. The intro prompt draws its why-this-org sentence from what they do today, never from an old thread it can still see.

- **2026-09-08 — The Outreach detail pane is one voice per zone.** Header says why the company is in front of you; "Where this stands" says only what the record says (Started / Agreed / Now / Next from `relationship_recap`); the draft card follows the CS card anatomy; the conversation is the archive. Colour is spent on state, never on kind.

- **2026-09-02 — Initiating messages are machine-drafted; continuations are operator-written.** The advisor drafts only messages we originate (`INITIATING_TYPES` in cadence.js), automatically in a nightly pass so the queue is review work. Tier-1 replies and directory revivals get no AI draft: reply drafts kept asserting facts outside the record, and line-by-line distrust costs more than writing the reply. Initiating drafts run on Sonnet deliberately (locked template bodies, system-assigned A/B subjects enforced by code override, tool-less advisor, operator review before send); anything reply-shaped stays Opus. The stored ai-body-vs-sent-body edit rate is the flip-back tripwire. A genuine inbound reply auto-dismisses any waiting initiating draft.

- **2026-09-03 — A held call is a queue signal, not a draft, and templates fill only what a lookup can answer.** The post-call entry opens the composer; a template types the boilerplate around the operator's own lines. Every template fill is deterministic with zero model calls, and bodies are Jamie's real sent mail. The call-notes placeholder is refused by the send tool with no bypass. The filled original is kept in `structured.template_body` so operator edits to a template are the same measurable signal as edits to an advisor draft.

- **2026-08-28 — An absent or unverified fact is not neutral; say which kind of nothing it is.** A null becomes "never contacted", a missing region becomes whatever a geocoder guesses, an empty facts block reads as "not a partner". So enrichment distinguishes `no_address` from `scrape_thin`, the advisor is told "they ARE a partner, records did not load" rather than nothing, and a geocode outside the stated country or region is refused as `conflict` rather than written. Klaviyo profile geolocation is never stored as org location.

- **2026-08-27 — The cadence owns when a company is next contacted, not the operator.** Partner check-ins are one October sitting at a flat 300-day threshold (seasonal windows were badly timed for these orgs and a rolling per-org date scattered the work). Snooze is deprecated: every real use meant "do not start something yet", which is the cadence's job; its reading path survives but nothing new can be set. Pause stays for the case no schedule can express. Reaching for a per-company override usually means the interval itself is wrong.

- **2026-08-27 — Any rule that removes rows from an operator queue needs a condition under which they come back.** Scheduled ladder sends are excluded from the queue (they are machine work, not a decision) but return badged stuck once pending past their slot, so an outage cannot be concealed by the exclusion.

- **2026-08-26 — The follow-up ladder anchors on the last chaseable outbound, and "have they answered?" is a question about that message.** `reply_close` is not a chaseable anchor. Scope is engine sends only (`source='send_tool'`) inside 90 days, enforced in the cadence: a manual Gmail send carries no message type, so chasing one means guessing what it was. A predicate about one message must be scoped to that message.

- **2026-08-26 — A cadence-set On Me claim carries a note; an operator-set one does not.** The ladder ends by retiring a lead or, for an active relationship, handing it to On Me (an unresponsive partner is not a dead lead, and does not need a fourth automated email). The note holds only durable facts (a count and a date); what to do still comes from `relationship_next_step`. `on_me_source` / `outreach_paused_source` keep cadence and operator claims distinguishable. Retiring is a cadence-set pause, never `relationship_state='lost'`, which would claim they said no.

- **2026-09-08 — A follow-up is fixed text, because nothing model-written goes out unreviewed.** The rungs shipped as auto-sent Opus drafts and produced pitches; Jamie reviews every customer-facing draft, so that was an exception nobody agreed to. Rungs now mirror the CS auto follow-up (rung 1 a one-liner, rung 2 quoting the chased message), both replies in the thread with zero model calls. Routing sits in `generateDraftForCompany` so the nightly pass, the panel and the console tool all get the template, and the advisor refuses a rung.

- **2026-08-26 — Unattended sending is guarded on targeting.** What goes wrong is who it reaches, so every guard is machine-checkable and runs immediately before the send: a forced Gmail re-check for a reply that landed since (inspecting the return value, since the discovery functions are fail-soft), an address-health check, and a daily cap. Scheduling into the recipient's mid-morning is what makes a click unnecessary: hours pass between drafting and sending, so the review window exists for free. Where the timezone is unknown we pick a time that is business hours in every zone the country spans rather than guess.

- **2026-08-26 — Attachment identity is per-file for uploads and per-kind for generated documents; `b2b_drafts.structured` is operator state.** The partnership agreement is a spec rendered fresh at send; operator uploads cannot be regenerated and are naturally plural, so they are keyed `upload:<storage path>`, with bytes stored at attach time so a refresh or failed send cannot lose them. `structured` holds every piece of operator state (attachments, To/Cc overrides, `facts_verified`, `next_touch_days`), so any path that composes a fresh row instead of updating the autosaved one discards it. Attachment size is checked against the total at send.

- **2026-08-24 — Mail from a person is not always a person writing, and "we are done here" is a thread close, not a deferral.** Calendar RSVPs and invitations arrive from the contact's own address and would otherwise read as Tier-1 replies; inbound classification is one function with a shared set of non-reply types. Detection is precision-first: an RSVP subject decides alone, an `Invitation:` subject must be corroborated, and no `Re:` tolerance anywhere. Closing a thread acts from the relationship block on the thread the queue entry means. It is deliberately not a fourth deferral: "nothing more to say until the next check-in" is precisely the case where the next check-in must still happen.

- **2026-08-20 — A claim about an action must be backed by the action.** A draft that says an invite was created is refused by `sendB2bEmail` unless a meeting row exists (fails open on a lookup error, with an `invite_created` bypass for the booking path), because the check must also cover the console, the MCP tool, and a draft sent after a page refresh. `skip_reply` books the event and sends no email for when they have already been told the time.

- **2026-08-20 — A delivery failure is work, not silence.** A bounce retires the dead address and lets recipient resolution fall through; `contact_unknown` is reserved for when nothing is reachable and is surfaced above the eligibility gate, because the gate is what would hide it. Recovery is a revival, not a regeneration: the approved `sent_body` returns as a new pending row while the original stays `sent`. Alternates are suggested, never used. `b2b_companies.last_outbound_at` is a denormalized copy and must be chased when a message is marked undelivered.

- **2026-08-20 — Scheduling shows the calendar rather than writing times into a draft.** An advisor told to propose times will invent them in customer-facing text, so the generated half was cut entirely: the panel is a read-only lookup and Jamie types the times. Removing the capability was cheaper than making it safe. Booking and telling them are inseparable (an event the other side was never told about is worse than no feature). Their timezone is a deterministic table that answers nothing for a multi-zone country with no region; Sonnet reads their proposed times but is kept out of offset arithmetic; unsociable-for-them slots are shown annotated rather than hidden. A booked call suppresses the cadence.

- **2026-08-19 — A contact change is one operation, not three edits.** Replace makes the new person the only primary, deactivates (never deletes) the named predecessor so their history still resolves, and clears `contact_unknown` together. Promote and retire are separate operations because the right person is usually already on file. An address registered to another company is refused rather than reassigned; retire refuses to remove the last reachable contact. Retired contacts stay visible under a "former" group with restore.

- **2026-08-19 — "We are not working this one" is our decision, not a relationship fact.** Pausing is an orthogonal pair (`outreach_paused_at` / `_reason`), never a `relationship_state` value: the company stays what it is and only our intent changes. Pause and snooze are one mechanism behind `deferredSince`. The rule that constrains every deferral: it clears a reply already sitting there and can never suppress one that arrives afterwards, so the comparison is against the moment the deferral was set, and a pause with no set-time suppresses nothing. Pause and snooze supersede the pending draft, and `mergePendingDraftEntries` (which re-adds any company holding a pending draft without consulting the cadence) independently excludes deferred companies. Verify a queue change against `fetchQueueWithDrafts`, never `fetchOutreachQueue`.

- **2026-08-20 / 2026-08-26 — On Me is a deferral where the work is still live.** Jamie owes them an answer but not today, so it keeps the draft and must exclude itself from the pending-draft merge. It carries days-on-you, a tab count and a digest section, because a relief valve with no ageing is a quieter backlog; what each claim is about comes from the relationship summary's next step rather than a note typed at claim time. A claim clears on send or Back to queue and nothing else (an acknowledgement reply must not erase an obligation it did not touch); a company can sit on both surfaces and the On Me row is badged "they replied since".

- **2026-08-19 — An absent record and an empty one are different answers.** The cadence read no messages as "never contacted", which is a confident wrong answer for companies whose history was never imported. The fix is to go and look (the nightly thread discovery sweep) rather than to teach the rule about nulls. Before a branch treats "no rows" as a fact about the world, something must have actually checked. The sweep withholds its searched-stamp when writes fail so a failed run cannot record itself as "looked, found nothing".

- **2026-08-13 — A shared Gmail thread is two relationships, so it gets two rows.** Gmail threads on subject, so one conversation regularly holds two orgs. Uniqueness is `(company_id, gmail_thread_id)`, every thread lookup is company-scoped, and status is per-row so Close/Reopen means something per relationship. `UNIQUE(external_id)` on anything sourced from a shared external system asserts the external entity belongs to exactly one of ours; when false it forces every writer into a silent overwrite. The bounce branch of `correlateInbound` stays unscoped because it is discovering the company, but refuses to guess when a thread has several owners.

- **2026-08-13 — A summariser's freshness test is a data boundary, not a wall clock.** History arrives backwards (thread discovery imports old threads long after a company is summarised), so the watermark is the `sent_at` of the newest message included plus a message count; a message landing behind it forces a full rebuild. The suggested next step is advisory and never writes `next_action_date`, because `cadence.js` owns what is due. The sheet-era `ai_summary` is retained as dated prologue. Summaries write every date absolutely, and the panel refreshes the summary on open (skipped while discovery is mid-flight) rather than relying on a staleness marker the operator has to notice.

- **2026-08-11 — Identity across the B2B stores is matched on domain, never on name.** The same org exists in `donation_partners`, `b2b_companies` and Gmail, and every name-based join has eventually fused two unrelated orgs. No match must mean no facts rather than a guess, because a wrong join silently puts one org's history into another org's email. `syncB2bCompanyState` still carries a name fallback (parked). An org missing from `b2b_companies` is invisible to the engine, so registry backfill is a correctness concern.

- **2026-08-11 — The advisor is given facts it would otherwise have to ask for.** Where our records can answer a question we are about to put to a partner (donation shipment counts and dates, the partner discount rate), the record goes in the context. The context states these are our send records, never confirmation of receipt, and a zero or low count is surfaced explicitly so the advisor acknowledges it.

- **2026-08-11 — One row per org per relationship_type, and the recipient must never depend on row order.** Duplicate rows silently break the panel's read of a relationship; merge on domain within relationship_type (a retailer and an org can legitimately share a domain). A cold opener is suppressed when a duplicate row for the same org is already engaged, guarded per company so the draft button cannot bypass it. `resolveRecipient` needs a total ordering, and where the correct pick is a human decision it must be stated in the data. `website` is not reliably a website (some rows hold free-mail or shortener domains), so domain matching needs a junk-domain denylist and a merge sweep must also cover rows with a null or junk website.

- **2026-08-11 — Inbound correlates on sender domain, not just the exact address, and what fails to correlate must be visible.** A sender whose identifying domain matches a company's website or an existing contact attaches, and the address is auto-registered non-primary so the exact match wins next time. Safe only because generic domains are excluded via `emailDomains.js`. A correlation step that can fail needs somewhere for its failures to land (now the inbound triage strip).

- **2026-08-07 — Thread membership is decided per message, not per thread.** A message is imported only when one of the company's known addresses appears in its from/to/cc. Our own address never counts as evidence of membership. Companies with no addresses on file are skipped rather than emptied.

- **2026-08-06 — The partnership agreement is generated, not written.** `donationAgreement.js` renders the LGBTQ+ Organization Donation Program agreement as a pre-signed PDF from a fixed HTML template with no AI anywhere: it is a contract, so clause wording must be byte-identical. The only variables are the org name and the discount, which is a country lookup (US/Australia 50%, elsewhere 30%, matching wholesale; the gap covers duties we absorb) with no per-partner exceptions, even where an agreement was once mis-issued at the wrong rate. The email around it stays advisor-written.

- **2026-08-06 — Never guess an email address; use the contact form.** Unverified addresses drive bounce rate toward the level that burns sender reputation on the same domain Klaviyo uses. `contact_form_url` is a first-class delivery channel: the draft is written normally, `sendB2bEmail` fails closed, and the panel hands over copy-paste text plus the link. The channel is derived, never stored (an email on file always wins). Delivery never affects tier; it only breaks ties so manual sends batch together.

- **2026-08-06 — One send path, whoever wrote the email.** The operator can compose directly, but the text is stored as a `b2b_drafts` row (`advisor: null`, which separates "Jamie wrote this" from "Jamie edited the AI's draft") and sent through the same path as an AI draft. Every send goes through `sendDraftById`; a second send path is how a row's state (attachments, overrides, `sent_body`) gets left behind, and the draft must be read before the recipient is resolved because a To override lives on it. The composer autosaves into that row, but never touches an advisor draft, whose `subject`/`body` paired with `sent_subject`/`sent_body` is the edit record.

- **2026-07-29 — `relationship_state` is not a funnel stage.** `prospect` means "never approached"; a company leaves it on any evidence of a relationship. Anything presenting a pipeline stage must derive it, and must keep relationship (account / lead / lost) separate from conversation (talking / closed / never); folding them into one filter hides active retailers whose threads have all concluded. `dormant` is still written by nothing, so `reactivation` remains unreachable (parked).

- **2026-08-05 — Tier-4 first-touch is admission-gated, not automatic.** Imported rows are not equally ready, so Tier 4 requires `vetted_at`, set per company by `b2b_triage` (keep / drop / snooze, no draft generated), and supply is let in cohort by cohort. If a future session finds Tier 4 quiet, admit a cohort; never remove the gate.

- **2026-08-05 — `$0` Shopify orders tagged `sample*` are a samples event, not revenue.** Purchases are price-driven; sample orders populate `samples_shipped_at`, bounded to 60 days so a backfilled historical sample cannot trigger a check-in late. Applies to any B2B revenue analysis, not just cadence.

- **2026-08-13 — The affiliate program does not exist and must never be offered.** The affiliate cadence types were removed as unreachable and no onboarding flow was ever built, yet the advisor prompts went on describing it as live. When a capability is cut, grep the prompts for it in the same change: the prompt is a product surface, and `facts_to_verify` is a review aid, not a guard. Cadence branches may only gate on context `buildContexts` actually sets; a test asserts the deleted types stay gone.

- **2026-08-11 — Within a tier, order must encode readiness.** Ready-to-send drafts sort first within a tier, still under Tier 1's oldest-waiting-person rule; a list with no meaningful secondary sort communicates a priority that does not exist.

- **2026-07-29 — The queue stays due-only; browsing is a separate surface.** The 6-tier queue answers "what needs action". Reaching a company the cadence hasn't surfaced is the directory's job, and drafting from there is explicitly operator-initiated (`force`), never a relaxation of the cadence. The advisor is told whether it is drafting a typed cadence message, an operator revival, or a genuine Tier-1 reply, because its default "they are waiting on us" framing is false for the first two.

- **2026-07-29 — Only the per-company view guarantees a complete conversation.** The queue-wide sweep skips closed threads (a concluded thread can't change what's due); the per-company sync includes them. Discovery never re-derives status on an existing thread, since the operator may have just closed or reopened it by hand.

- **2026-07-24 — Reorder cadence is frequency-aligned, not flat.** Nudge threshold is 0.75 × the company's latest order interval, clamped 90-365d, computed by the daily sync (last interval beats median/mean because bursty histories lie both ways). The advisor can override timing per send via `next_touch_days` when the thread gives a concrete reason, bounded 7-365d; the operator sees the override before sending.

- **Never manually parse CSV data** — always pass raw CSV to `parse_wholesale_input`.

- **Order creation tool selection:** `create_wholesale_order` requires an existing Shopify customer ID; use it for established wholesale accounts. For one-off bulk or community orders where the customer may not exist yet, use `create_order` with `discount_percent: 50`, which finds or creates the customer.

- **Two-tier discovery:** Google Maps for breadth, then deep research for depth. Merge sources when a domain already exists rather than duplicating.

- **Shipping speed is driven by the Shopify shipping method title.** All order-creation tools take `shipping_speed: 'standard' | 'expedited'`; the title (`getShippingMethodTitle` in `orderUtils.js`) is the entire carrier + incoterms signal that Warehance matches on, so no FedEx tags. Wholesale defaults: standard for US, expedited for non-US. A real shipping charge (`shipping_price`) may only ride an expedited title, because every standard rate is named "Free … Shipping"; charging via a custom line item is a trap since `discount_percent` applies per line. Partner incoterms override the zone default by picking the other title.

- **Shopify draft-order discount amounts are in the order's base currency, never the presentment currency.** Any fixed-amount discount (e.g. `credit_items` comps) must be computed in USD, or a presentment-currency figure on an AUD order over-credits by the FX factor. The AU de minimis split is decided in AUD whatever the invoice currency: a forced-currency partner is probed in AUD at Shopify's live rate, then re-created in USD one draft per split, and those orders split against a threshold with an FX margin.

- **`pre_increase_pricing` flag on `create_wholesale_order`:** per-line prices use `price_history.previous_price` from the April 2026 rollout row × country discount instead of current retail; SKUs without a rollout row fall back to current retail silently, and no draft-level discount is applied. Remove the flag once all partners have transitioned (park a cleanup entry then).
