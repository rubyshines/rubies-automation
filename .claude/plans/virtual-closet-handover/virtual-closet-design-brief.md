## What the Virtual Closet is

RUBIES makes gender-affirming underwear and swimwear for trans girls and women (rubyshines.com). LGBTQ+ community centres run "closets" that give gender-affirming gear to people who need it. Those closets are well stocked with binders and thin on everything else.

The Virtual Closet is a page in a centre's name where its community stocks a closet of RUBIES underwear and swimwear, and RUBIES matches every dollar. The centre shares one link. Nothing to maintain, nothing to pay. Three doors on that page:

1. **Buy.** 20% off a first order at rubyshines.com, applied automatically at checkout. For every two pairs bought through the page, the closet gets one.
2. **Apply for a free pair.** Anyone can ask for what they need, in their size. It comes with the closet's next shipment.
3. **Sponsor the closet.** Give the price of an item into the closet's next shipment. RUBIES matches every dollar.

Everything lands in one box per centre. When it reaches its goal it ships as double the product ($300 raised becomes $600 of product), and everyone who asked gets theirs at the same time.

The app around that page has three audiences: the public (the closet page and the programme page with sign-up), the centre's team (a private view by magic link), and the RUBIES operator (a login).

### The two programmes, one sign-up

A centre signs up once and ticks what it wants:

- **Pre-Loved partner** (working name, may change): customers' exchanged and returned RUBIES items are routed to the centre's closet. RUBIES already runs this with partners on three continents. The centre appears on a public map of donation partners.
- **Virtual Closet**: the page and box described above.

Any approved partner can also **order for its closet at any time** with its own budget (a grant, year-end money) at **50% off, $300 minimum**. This is a standing capability, not a tick box. A centre can also **add any amount to its box**, where it is matched like every other dollar and ships with the box.

Sign-up is self-service. The centre fills in the form, accepts the terms, and lands on a confirmation immediately. The operator approves from a queue (usually the same day) before the page and map listing go live. "Book a call with Jamie" is offered on the programme page and the confirmation, and is optional.

## Vocabulary and copy rules (apply on every screen)

- **Matching, never discount, with one exception.** Anything about the box says "RUBIES matches every dollar", whoever put the money in. Never "discount", "wholesale", "closet price" or "doubled" about the box, on any centre- or sponsor-facing surface. **The exception is an order the centre places itself** ("order now"): that is a purchase, and it says "50% off, $300 minimum" plainly. The only percentage a community member sees is the shopper's 20% off.
- **Community-facing copy says "shipment"; centre-facing copy says "box".** The community page never says "box".
- **Buyers see one sentence** about the closet's benefit: "for every two pairs bought through this page, the closet gets one". The number 25% never appears on the community page.
- **Requesters see timing only**: "comes with the closet's next shipment". No dollars, no goal, no mechanism.
- **Sponsors see dollars raised of a goal**, like a fundraiser ("$88 raised of $300"), tiles priced per item, and one footnote: "RUBIES matches every dollar, so $16 becomes a $32 pair."
- **The centre sees the mechanics**, in the programme page terms and in its own view.
- **Never label the reader.** Say what the product is ("no-tuck underwear and swimwear", "gender-affirming underwear and swimwear"), never who is holding the page.
- **Never say "platform", "app", "sign up for the app" or "prototype" to a centre.** What a centre experiences is a page with its name that raises gear for its closet.
- **Plus sizes are 1X, 2X, 3X, 4X** in anything a community member sees, never XL, 2XL.
- **No em dashes** anywhere. Use commas, parentheses or short sentences.
- **They/them** for any person whose pronouns are not stated. Requesters give "the name you'd like us to use", never a legal name.
- **Never "patented" or "patent-pending".**
- **Voice:** playful but respectful, confident but approachable, supportive, never political, righteous or judgmental.
- Free pairs are final; free exchanges are a benefit of buying. The request form says so before anyone submits.

## Who uses it, and each one's flow (draw these on the Flows page)

**Shopper** (someone who can buy). Opens the centre's link, lands on the closet page, taps "Shop with 20% off", is sent to rubyshines.com with the discount applied silently, shops and checks out as normal ("Discount applied" at checkout). Nothing else to design on the store side. The order credits the closet.

**Requester** (someone who cannot buy right now). Opens the link, fills the request form (name they go by, items, pickup or ship, why, email), sees "Request sent" with timing. Receives a confirmation email. If the current shipment is already funded, the confirmation says they are on the list for the next one and how many are ahead. When the shipment goes out: a pickup notice ("ready at [centre]") or a shipped notice with tracking. If declined by a centre that approves manually: a kind note.

**Sponsor** (someone who wants to help). Opens the link, reads the sponsor section, picks a tile, is sent to rubyshines.com checkout for a non-shipping "Sponsor [centre]'s closet" product, pays, receives a thank-you naming the centre. The closet page's dollars raised update.

**Centre team.** Reads the programme page, signs up (form, terms, confirmation), waits for approval, receives the welcome email with the link and the share kit. Opens its private view by magic link. Sees the current box (raised of goal, requests), changes settings, shares the link. If approving manually: gets an email per request with Approve and Decline links. When the box reaches its goal: gets a "box is ready" email, opens the order builder pre-filled with requested items, fills the rest or lets it auto-fill, sends it. Can also add its own budget to the box (matched) or place an order anytime at 50% off. Receives a monthly statement when something happened.

**RUBIES operator** (one person, Jamie). Logs in. Sees the queue of new sign-ups, approves, asks for more information, or declines. Sees the list of all centres with health, opens any centre's detail, and a programme dashboard with totals. Also receives the alerts (a wait list growing, a centre whose volume jumps, an unclaimed sign-up sitting for days).

8. From sign-up to your first box, four steps: sign up here (five minutes: your address, the sizes you offer, your limits, your target, a logo); we approve, usually the same day; your page goes live and we send everything to share, plus a starter run of AJ underwear for the closet; the first box fills and at $300 we ship, doubled.

10. Contact: Jamie Alexander, founder, jamie@rubyshines.com.

### Centre (private view, reached by magic link)

**C0. Sign in.** Email field, "Send me a link". Confirmation: "Check your email." No passwords.

**C2. Centre home: the current box.** Header with the centre's name and a link to its public page. Box [N]: "$88 raised of $300", progress, the same counts as the public card, and three actions: "Send it now" (enabled from $300), "Add to the box" (an amount from the centre's budget, matched) and "Order now, 50% off" (C5). **C3. Settings.** Virtual Closet: sizes offered, items per request, how often one person may ask, funding target ($300 floor), approval mode, allow shipping to a door, pause requests. ### Operator (RUBIES, one login)

**O1. Login.** Email and password, or a magic link; either is fine, note the choice.

**O2. Queue.** New sign-ups waiting: centre name, city, programmes ticked, website (link), logo preview, description, geocode result on a small map, contact, submitted date, "asked for a call" flag. Actions: Approve (goes live, welcome email sent), Ask for more information (opens a short message), Decline (with a reason). Also in the queue: anything else needing a human: a wait list growing past a threshold, a centre whose order volume jumped, a sign-up sitting more than three days, an out-of-stock item on a box about to ship.

**O3. Centres.** A table of every centre: name, region, programmes, status (pending, active, paused, left), current box raised of goal, requests waiting, community orders this month, last activity, a health flag. Filters by programme, status, region. Search.

**O4. Centre detail.** Everything about one centre: contacts, settings as the centre set them, the public page link, the box ledger (every dollar in and out, with source), requests (all, with status), link clicks and orders (codes issued, used, conversion, geography), sponsors, boxes sent, Pre-Loved donations routed, statements sent, notes. Actions: pause, message the centre, edit settings on their behalf, send the box on their behalf, mark the starter run shipped.

**O5. Programme dashboard.** Totals across all centres, this month and all time: active centres, dollars raised, boxes shipped, items to closets, requests filled, community orders, sponsors, conversion from link to order. A small per-centre table sorted by activity. A short "needs attention" list (the same items as the queue's second half).

## What to leave out

- No lobby stand, cards, QR kits or printed pieces (parked).
- No leaderboard or ranking of centres.
- No per-request "cover this person" sponsoring; money goes into the box, never to a named request.
- No shared or visible discount code anywhere.
- No inventory the centre maintains, no intake tooling for the centre's clients.
- Nothing on the community page that says "prototype", "demo" or "example".
- No fake phone status bars or keyboards in the phone frames.

- Whether a centre's own "add to the box" and "order now" are paid on the spot or by invoice.
