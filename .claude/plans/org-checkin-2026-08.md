# LGBTQ+ Org Check-In Round — August 2026

Re-engagement sweep across all 18 active donation partners. Goal is a reply, not a survey.

## The five asks (Jamie's brief)

1. How are they doing?
2. Are they receiving RUBIES packages?
3. Any feedback on the program or products from their community?
4. Any change to the primary contact?
5. Reminder they can purchase (50% US + Australia, 30% everywhere else)

## Shape decisions

- **Ask about packages with our number in hand.** We know exactly what we routed and when. "We've sent 13 packages since May, most recently Aug 4" is a question they can answer in one line. "Are you getting packages?" is a question they have to go research, which is how a check-in dies.
- **One real question, then short beats.** The five asks are a lot for a re-approach. Lead with the relationship, put contact-check and purchase reminder as one-liners at the end.
- **No purchase pressure.** These are donation partners, not accounts. TransPonder was explicitly told in Sept 2025 that regular purchases are not required to stay in the program. The reminder is an open door, not a pitch.
- **Outside the US, name the low volume before they do.** It reads as honesty rather than an excuse, and it heads off the awkwardness of a check-in about a program that has sent them one package.
- **Don't volunteer the 50 vs 30 comparison.** State their rate. The duty explanation only belongs where it's already in that thread's history.
- **No em dashes** (brand rule). Jamie's voice: short paragraphs, warm, direct. Sign-off "Talk soon, / Jamie Alexander, RUBIES Founder".
