Virtual Closet

A gender-affirming closet your community stocks, in your centre's name, with every dollar matched by RUBIES.

The gap

Closets are strong on binders and thin on everything else

Centres tell us the same thing: requests for trans-fem and non-binary gear come in, budgets are small, and binder brands have been cutting community programmes. Nobody covers the other half of the closet.

Demand you already see

Walk-ins and request forms ask for underwear and swimwear, and go home with a binder or nothing.

Budgets that don't stretch

A closet's whole gear budget for a year buys a few dozen items at retail.

A community that wants to help

Allies, families and members ask how. There is rarely a simple answer.

The idea

One page in your name. Your community buys, asks and gives. Everything lands in one box.

Every dollar that reaches the box is matched by RUBIES. When the box is full it ships to your closet, and everyone who asked gets theirs at the same time.

2×every dollar, matched by RUBIES
Three doors

Those who can buy

Apply for a free pair

Those who want to help

Sponsor the closet
