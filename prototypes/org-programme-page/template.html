Closets are strong on binders and thin on everything else

Centres tell us the same thing: requests for trans-fem and non-binary gear come in, budgets are small, and binder brands have been cutting community programmes. The Virtual Closet is the other half of the closet, stocked by the people around it.

One page in your name, three ways in

Your community buys, asks and gives. Everything lands in one box. When the box is full it ships to your closet, and everyone who asked gets theirs at the same time.

Those who can buy

Buy a pair, 20% off

Your centre's link gives 20% off a first order at RUBIES, and for every two pairs bought, your closet gets one.

Those who can't right now

Apply for a free pair

Anyone can ask for what they need, in their size. You approve it from an email. It is filled when the box goes out.

Those who want to help

Sponsor the closet

Allies and families give any amount into the box, and RUBIES matches every dollar.

The terms

The matchRUBIES matches every dollar that reaches your box, from sponsors, from community orders, or from your own budget.
The boxYou set the funding target, $300 minimum. At the target it ships as double the product, so $300 becomes $600. If approved requests add up to more than the target, the goal grows to cover everyone. Past the goal you choose: send it now, or keep raising and send a bigger one. One box is open at a time; when it ships the next opens.
Community ordersYour link gives new customers 20% off their first order. Each person gets their own single-use code by email; there is no shared code to spread. For every two pairs bought through your link, your closet gets one: 25% of each order goes into the box and is matched like every other dollar there, so a $32 pair puts $16 of product in your closet.
Free pairsSomeone asks with the name they go by, what they need, and a sentence on what it would mean for them. Requests are approved automatically, or by you from an email if you prefer. You set how many items a request may hold, how often one person may ask, and the sizes you offer. Free pairs are filled when the box goes out and are final; if a shipment is already funded, a new request joins the wait list for the next one, in order. If an item is out of stock when the box ships, the closest style in that size is swapped in.
Pick up or shipPeople choose. Pickups come out of your box. A pair shipped to someone's door costs your box $15 for shipping. Your team sees the name, the items and the words, so you can hand pairs over. Email and address stay with RUBIES.
What fills the boxFive styles to start: AJ, Charlie and Sassy underwear, the Brooke bra, and the Ruby bikini bottom, in the sizes you offer. Requested items first. You choose the rest, or let it fill itself with what your community buys most.
Cost to your centreNothing. No minimum purchase, no reporting. Leave whenever you like; anything raised ships to you.

  • The 12 buyers each saved $6.40, and between them put six pairs in the closet without doing anything else.
