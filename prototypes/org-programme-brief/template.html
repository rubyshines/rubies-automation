The terms

The matchRUBIES matches every dollar that reaches your closet's shipment, whoever gave it.
The shipmentYou set the target, $300 minimum. At the target it becomes double the product, so $300 becomes $600, in the sizes you choose, and everyone who asked gets theirs. If more people ask, the goal grows to cover them. Past it you decide: send now, or keep raising.
Free pairsApproved automatically, with limits you set. Picked up at your closet or shipped to them. Anyone beyond a funded shipment joins the list for the next. Free pairs are final.
Cost to your centreNothing. No minimum, no reporting. Leave whenever you like; anything raised ships to you.
