5. International Shipping & DDP Costs

Fulfillment Model

RUBIES fulfillment: Products manufactured in China, bulk-shipped to a US warehouse (duty paid on bulk import). US orders (~70% of revenue) fulfilled domestically — no per-order DDP costs. Non-US orders ship via Passport from the US, with RUBIES covering DDP (duties, taxes, clearance).

US Bulk Import Duties

  • Paid on bulk China → US warehouse shipments

  • This is a structural advantage vs competitors shipping individual parcels internationally

Implications for Pricing

DDP is NOT the primary driver for price increases. At ~$27K/year, DDP costs are manageable. Adjusted gross margin is actually healthy at 72.6%. The real drivers are: (1) being significantly underpriced vs competitors (most charge $32-55 for tucking underwear), (2) the $1M CAD revenue target, and (3) building margin headroom for new product launches and wholesale growth. International pricing adjustments (+15%) are recommended to capture fair value in those markets.

Volume Discount Restructure

Standardize the existing AJ/Ruby volume discounts across all products:

Accessibility commitment preserved: With the volume discount, loyal customers buying 3+ items actually pay near or below current prices ($28.80 vs $28). This protects your mission of accessibility while increasing revenue from one-off and two-item buyers.

Discount Hygiene

  • Keep: Welcome-10, Birthday-10, volume tiering (restructured above)
  • Sunset: Deep percentage-off sales. Replace with "free item" or "free shipping" promos.
  • For sale events: Cap at 15% sitewide, or use tiered spend-to-save (spend $100 = 10%, spend $150 = 15%)
  • 58% discount rate is high. Goal: reduce to ~40% while maintaining revenue through higher base prices
