Implications for Pricing

DDP is part of a larger shipping cost problem. DDP alone is $27K/year, but when combined with 3PL fulfillment costs (see Section 10), total shipping losses are ~$56K+/year. This makes shipping the second-largest hidden cost after COGS. The price increases and shipping rate adjustments recommended in this report address both sides: higher retail prices increase per-order margin to absorb shipping costs, while rate adjustments reduce the per-order shipping loss directly.

Shipping Pricing Recommendations

A. Keep US Threshold at $99 — But Acknowledge the Real Cost

The $99 threshold drives behavior — free shipping jumps from 7.4% to 76.9% at $99 — but it costs you money. Every free-shipping US order costs you ~$7.35 with zero recovery. With 6,029 free US orders in the dataset, that's ~$44K in unrecovered fulfillment costs. The threshold is still the right move (it drives 2x AOV), but you should know the true cost. Do not lower it. The price increase (+$4/item) effectively offsets the per-order shipping loss.

B. Add an International Free Shipping Threshold: $125

Currently: no free shipping threshold for DDP/DDU zones. Every international customer pays $12 regardless of cart size. Adding a $125 threshold would:
  • Incentivize add-ons (a $48 bikini + $42 bra = $90 → add a $32 underwear to hit $122, close to threshold)
  • Offset DDP losses: a $125+ international order with $20 DDP cost still has $90+ margin after COGS + DDP + shipping
  • Match TomboyX's international free shipping threshold
Estimated cost: ~$8-12K/year in forgone shipping revenue on high-value intl orders
Estimated gain: +$15-25K from higher intl AOV + better conversion

Why Experiment?

You have strong instincts and data, but some pricing questions can only be answered by testing. The goal: make small, reversible changes, measure the impact, and iterate. Never bet the whole business on one price point.

Annual Price Review Process

Establish a yearly pricing review (January, before spring/summer peak):

  • Frequency: Every January — review and adjust prices for the year ahead
  • Default increase: 2-5% to keep pace with inflation and COGS changes
  • No announcement needed: Just update prices. Customers rarely notice small incremental increases.
  • Inputs to review: COGS trends, competitor pricing (re-scan annually), Passport DDP costs, conversion rate by product, customer feedback
  • Your competitors do this: LeoLines raised prices enough that Sock Drawer Heroes dropped them. UNTAG and Origami have both increased over the past 2 years.
  • This initial increase (~12%) is a catch-up after years of never raising prices. Future annual increases of 2-5% will be much less noticeable.
