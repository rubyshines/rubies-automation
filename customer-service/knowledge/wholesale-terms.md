# Wholesale Terms

## Pricing
- **US & Australia:** 50% off retail price
- **All other countries:** 30% off retail price

## Shipping
- Free shipping on all wholesale orders.

## Order Minimums
- Contact us for current minimum order requirements.

## Australian Orders
- Orders over $1,000 AUD are automatically split into multiple shipments to stay under the customs de minimis threshold.

## Currency
- Wholesale orders are processed in the customer's local currency by default.
- Exception: Sock Drawer Heroes (hello@sockdrawerheroes.com) is always invoiced in USD.

## How to Order
- Wholesale orders can be placed via email or through our wholesale portal.
- Include: product styles, colors, sizes, and quantities.
- Orders are created as draft invoices for review before payment.

## Payment
- Draft order invoices are sent via Shopify.
- Payment is collected before shipping.
