# RUBIES Sizing Guide

## IMPORTANT: Sizing Changed End of 2023

Previously, RUBIES used youth-oriented sizing only, spanning 4-24. At the end of 2023, we refined our sizing into two systems to better serve our diverse community of kids, teens, and adults:

- **Youth Sizes:** 4-16 (kids and teens)
- **Adult Sizes:** XS-4XL

**Conversations about sizing from before 2024 may reference the old system.** Always verify against current sizing below.

## Current Sizing System (2024+)

### Youth Sizes
Sizes: 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16

### Adult Sizes
Sizes: XS, XS+, S, M, L, 1X (XL), 2X (XXL), 3X (3XL), 4X (4XL)

### Products by Sizing Type
- **Youth + Adult sizing:** AJ, Charlie, Brooke, Ruby (available in both)
- **Adult sizing only:** Ava, Cheeky, Sassy

## Legacy → Current Size Conversion

For customers transitioning from the old youth sizes (pre-2024):

| Previous Youth Size | New Adult Size |
|--------------------|----------------|
| 12                 | XS             |
| 13                 | XS+            |
| 14                 | S              |
| 16                 | M              |
| 18                 | L              |
| 20                 | XL (1X)        |
| 22                 | 2XL (2X)       |
| 24                 | 3XL (3X)       |
| 26                 | 4XL (4X)       |

Youth sizes 12, 13, 14, and 16 are exactly the same as adult sizes XS, XS+, S, and M. Customers in this range can order either youth or adult sizing.

For a while, the website lists old youth sizes next to new adult sizes (e.g., "L/18" or "XL/20").

## Common Size Aliases
- XL = 1X
- XXL = 2X
- 3XL = 3X
- 4XL = 4X
- 5XL = 5X

## Fit Notes
- **Between sizes?** Generally recommend sizing up for comfort.
- **Too tight in waist:** Try one size up.
- **Too loose overall:** Try one size down.
- **AJ and Charlie** have the most structured fit with a wide waistband.
- **Ava** is a bikini brief style — runs slightly smaller in the hip area.
- **Cheeky** has less coverage — consider this when sizing.
- **Plus sizes (1X+)** are designed with more room through the hip and thigh.
