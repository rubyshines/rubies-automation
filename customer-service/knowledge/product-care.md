# Product Care Instructions

## Washing
- Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle.
- Use a mesh laundry bag to protect the garment.
- Do not use bleach or harsh detergents.

## Drying
- Lay flat or hang to dry.
- Do not tumble dry — heat can damage the elastic and fabric.

## Storage
- Store flat or folded — avoid hanging for long periods.

## General
- RUBIES underwear is designed for everyday wear and durability.
- Following care instructions helps maintain the shape, color, and elastic life of your garment.
