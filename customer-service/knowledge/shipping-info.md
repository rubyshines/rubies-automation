# Shipping Information

## Domestic (Australia)
- Standard shipping available Australia-wide.
- Tracking provided via email once shipped.

## International
- We ship internationally to most countries.
- International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the buyer.
- Delivery times vary by destination.

## Tracking
- All orders receive tracking numbers via email once dispatched.
- Check your spam/junk folder if you haven't received tracking.
- For tracking issues, provide your order number and we can look it up.

## Wholesale Shipping
- Wholesale orders ship free.
- Australian wholesale orders over $1,000 AUD are automatically split into multiple shipments to stay under the de minimis threshold.
