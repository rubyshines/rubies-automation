## Product facts
- ALL tops (bras and swim) have a built-in pouch/pocket for breast pads; pads are movable, no tacking.
- Chest pads come in sets of 3 and are layered to build shape. For subtle AA/A looks recommend M + layering. Gel pads are fine for occasional swimming (limit heavy chlorine exposure). Large gel pads restock mid-August; foam pads are the in-stock alternative.
- Tankini (Queeny) has NO tall version; only the Sky one-piece does. Large Tall is still too short for very tall torsos — steer to separates.
- AJ and Ruby cuts are similar; Charlie has a slightly higher rise than AJ; AJ ≈ Charlie cut minus bow/scalloped edges. Charlie pairs as a matching set with the Brooke bra.
- Cheeky is designed for swim; as underwear it "works in a pinch" only.
- Ava and Brooke offer similar coverage/stealth (depends on outer top). One set includes THREE sets of pads.
- No white underwear (white isn't good for shaping); unicorn print is youth sizes only.
- All numeric sizes (4–16) are YOUTH sizes.
- Swimwear and underwear are cut to different widths (different fabric stretch).
- Genesis surf shorts: redesigned for better shaping, ready ~end of August, preorder via newsletter.
- Naomi gaff: first Canada run sold out; production moving to China (sampling now); restock ~fall/end of year; volume discount planned once supply is stable.
- Flo redesign (higher leg openings) exists but waits for current inventory to sell.
- Queeny 2X garment length = 67 cm (garment specs exist — should be tool-accessible).
- New colorways blocked by supplier MOQs; non-core colors sell slowly and carry warehouse cost — honest small-business explanation, not "I'll pass it along".
- For pale skin recommend Sandstone as the versatile color.
- Ruby product page has the most photos; Ruby is constructed similarly inside to Cheeky — use as visual reference.

## Process rules Jamie applies
- Invoice the price difference on cross-product exchanges (and refund the difference when downgrading); hold stays until the customer confirms the invoice is paid.
- A new purchase CAN be merged into an exchange order + one invoice for the difference (create_invoice_order exchange_items + paid_items).
- Wrong-item-received: ask for a hang-tag photo (distinguishes warehouse mis-pick from supplier mislabel); ship replacement expedited.
- Stalled/lost package playbook: confirm ship-to address, set a concrete wait-until date, pre-commit to reship/refund after that date. Carrier "delivered but missing": packages often turn up within a week.
- One-piece sizing: waist (at belly + ~1 inch under belly button) + height. Two-piece top/bra: chest where the band sits. Fabric delta ≈ 2"/size on waist only (never band unless verified via get_fabric_delta).
